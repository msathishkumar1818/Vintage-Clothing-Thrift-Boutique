Verification Protocols

How Every Garment Is Graded & Archived

Transparency is our core principle. Every piece in our boutique is evaluated under specialized textile standards before acceptance.

V-01

Era & Provenance Trace

Identifying manufacturer labels, union marks, font typography, and zipper brands.

V-02

Textile Tensile Test

Inspecting seam load, warp and weft stability, and natural fiber elasticity.

V-03

Steam & Ozone Purify

Non-chemical deep sanitation preserving fragile dye pigments and natural oils.

V-04

Permanent Vault Tagging

Serial registration with exact dimensions, care regime, and authenticity warranty.
