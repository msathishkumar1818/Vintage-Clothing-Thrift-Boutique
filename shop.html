Fit Accuracy Promise

How to Read Vintage Measurements

Vintage tag sizes (like a 1970s size 12) rarely match modern vanity sizing. That’s why we hand-measure every item laid completely flat.

Chest / Pit-to-Pit

Armpit seam to armpit seam

Double this number to get bust . Compare with your best-fitting favorite jacket.

Waist Band

Straight across top waistband

Since vintage denim has zero elastane/spandex, rise naturally sits on your body.

Full Drop Length

High shoulder point to bottom hem

Ensures coats, maxi dresses, and slips hang exactly at ankle or knee length.
