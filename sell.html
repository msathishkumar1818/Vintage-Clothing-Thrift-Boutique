The Simple Process

How Mail-In & In-Person Selling Works

Whether you visit our boutique fitting parlor or consign from home across the country, selling to us is seamless.

1

Submit Quick Photos Online

Snap clear photos of front, back, and inner labels. Our curators review within 4 business hours.

2

Receive Prepaid Insured Mailer Kit

We ship a sturdy water-resistant consignment box with prepaid tracking labels directly to your door.

3

In-Hand Appraisal & Final Guarantee

Our master textile conservators physically authenticate fibers, zippers, and condition grade.

4

Instant Cash or Credit Transfer

Approve your offer and receive immediate payment. If you decline, we ship your items back free of charge.
