The Physical Spaces

Designed for Tactile Exploration

We built our storefront as an antidote to clinical retail. Explore three distinct dedicated spaces inside our parlor.

Salon Space 01 · Velvet Suites

The Private Fitting Chambers

Spacious curtained parlors with natural daytime daylight balance, full-length three-way vintage mirrors, and vintage silk robes.

Salon Space 02 · Vinyl & Espresso

The Analog Listening Bar

Listen to curated 1970s soul and 80s post-punk on vinyl while savoring complimentary pour-over Ethiopian roast coffee.

Salon Space 03 · Authentication Desk

The Curator Appraisal Counter

Bring your vintage garments directly to our counter. Watch our textile conservators authenticate and appraise in real time.
